dmulife

A native macOS app for tasks, calendar and daily planning.

dmulife brings your tasks, calendars and the messages that turn into work into one place, and helps you plan each day around the time you actually have.

Features

  • Tasks with estimates, deadlines, projects and tags, planned onto days and timeboxed onto your calendar.
  • Google and iCloud calendars side by side with your tasks; create and edit events without leaving the app.
  • An inbox for starred or labelled Gmail threads and Slack mentions and marked messages, so you can turn them into tasks.
  • Daily planning and shutdown rituals, weekly objectives and reflections.
  • Optional AI assistance with your own Anthropic API key, off by default.
  • Sync between your Macs through your own iCloud account.

Availability

dmulife is in private beta on macOS 26 and later, distributed through TestFlight.

Your data

dmulife has no server of its own. Your data stays on your Mac and in your iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.
